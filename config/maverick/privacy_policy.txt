MAVERICKSTATION PRIVACY POLICY (version %POLICY_VERSION%)

We want as little of your data as we can get away with. This page tells you what we collect, why we collect it, and how long we keep it.

WHO WE ARE
MaverickStation is a small, private Space Station 13 server. Leadership runs it. We do not sell your data, we do not share it, and we do not show ads.

WHAT WE COLLECT, AND WHY
1. Your BYOND key, IP address and computer ID. BYOND sends these to every server you join. We use them to run bans and to stop ban evasion.
2. Connection records. We record when you connect, with the data in item 1.
3. Game logs. The server records what happens in a round: speech, OOC, PDA messages, emotes, fights and admin actions. Admins use these logs to handle reports.
4. Your characters and preferences. We save them so they load next time.
5. Your age check result. We keep "passed", or a ban if you did not pass. We never store your date of birth. Your game client works out your age and sends only that number.
6. Your recent connections. Your game window keeps your last 5 connections (key, IP address, computer ID) on your computer. It sends them to us when you join. We use them only to find alt accounts and ban evasion.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO
- We do not ask for ID, photos or your real name.
- We do not sell or share your data.
- We do not publish logs.
- We do not send your data to outside services.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT
- Game logs and connection records: %LOG_RETENTION_DAYS% days. The server deletes older records automatically.
- Bans: until the ban is lifted. A ban keeps the key and computer ID it applies to.
- Your account record (key, last IP address, last computer ID, first and last join date): while the server runs, unless you ask us to delete it.
- Characters and preferences: until you delete them or ask us to.
- Your age check result and this agreement: while the server runs.

YOUR CHOICES
You can ask leadership to show you your data or to delete it. We can keep a ban record, and logs that are part of an open report, until that matter is closed.
Contact: to be announced.

ADULTS ONLY
You must be %MINIMUM_AGE% or older to play here. If we find out that you are not, we ban you and delete your data, except the ban record.

BYOND
BYOND is a separate service with its own privacy policy. We do not control what BYOND collects.

CHANGES
If we change this policy in a way that matters, we ask you to agree again the next time you join.
